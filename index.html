Generative Attacks on LLMs

Deep generative modeling for adversarial prompt synthesis and LLM robustness.

More about this project

Used deep generative models to synthesize adversarial prompts that probe robustness and failure modes of large language models. The project studied how generated prompts behave under different safety filters and what kinds of mitigation strategies can improve robustness.

The work combined PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, and experiment-driven evaluation.

Hessian-Free Optimization

Conjugate-gradient methods for convex optimization from scratch.

More about this project

Implemented conjugate-gradient and Hessian-free optimization methods from scratch, targeting numerical accuracy and practical speedups over standard solver baselines.

The experiments reached optimality gaps at or below 1e-6 and were applied to portfolio optimization and sparse regression tasks.
